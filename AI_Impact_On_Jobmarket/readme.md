# AI Impact on Jobs & Future Skills 🤖

## About the Project
This project analyses how Artificial Intelligence may impact jobs, industries, employment trends, and future skill requirements.

It uses MySQL for data analysis and Microsoft Power BI to create interactive dashboards.

## Aim
To understand the potential impact of AI on employment, identify occupations with automation risks, and explore future job opportunities and in-demand skills.

## Tools Used
- **MySQL** – Data storage, SQL queries, and analysis.
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboards and visualisation.

## Dataset
- 8 Countries
- 10 Industries
- 40 Occupations
- 25 Skills
- 2025–2035 data

## Dashboard Pages

**1. Global AI Impact & Industry Overview**
- AI Exposure by Industry
- Automation Risk
- AI Augmentation Potential
- Employment Trends
- AI Adoption by Country

**2. Job Risk & Future Opportunities**
- High-Risk Occupations
- AI-Augmented Occupations
- Employment Growth
- Future Skills Demand
- Skill Demand Trends

## Dataset Disclaimer
The dataset is synthetic and intended for educational and analytical purposes. Results represent illustrative scenarios, not official statistics or guaranteed predictions.
